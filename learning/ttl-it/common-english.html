📚 台酒高頻核心字彙庫（附音標、繁中翻譯與發音）

HIGH-FREQUENCY VOCABULARY BANK

以下精選台酒與國營事業近年出題率最高之 30 組核心高頻字彙，涵蓋企業管理、供應鏈、資訊科技與永續經營：

compliance /kəmˈplaɪəns/
名詞 · 合規、法規遵循、順從
The act of adhering to official rules, specifications, or laws
例句： The corporation must ensure full compliance with the latest environmental protection laws.
【翻譯】該企業必須確保完全遵循最新的環境保護法規。
【高頻考法】in compliance with (依照、符合)
sustainable /səˈsteɪnəbəl/
形容詞 · 永續的、可持續的
Able to be maintained at a certain rate or level without depleting resources
例句： TTL is committed to promoting sustainable packaging and reducing carbon emissions.
【翻譯】台灣菸酒致力於推廣永續包裝並降低碳排放。
【高頻考法】sustainable development (永續發展)
logistics /ləˈdʒɪstɪks/
名詞 · 物流、後勤運籌
The detailed coordination of a complex operation involving many people, facilities, or supplies
例句： Efficient logistics management plays a vital role in nationwide product distribution.
【翻譯】高效的物流管理在全國產品經銷配送中扮演關鍵角色。
【高頻考法】supply chain and logistics (供應鏈與物流)
authenticate /ɔːˈθɛntɪˌkeɪt/
動詞 · 驗證、證實真實性
To prove or show something to be true, genuine, or valid
例句： Users must authenticate their identities using two-factor authentication before accessing the portal.
【翻譯】使用者在登入入口網站前，必須使用雙因子認證驗證其身份。
【高頻考法】authentication vs. authorization (認證 vs 授權)
fluctuation /ˌflʌktʃuˈeɪʃən/
名詞 · 波動、起伏變動
An irregular rising and falling in number or amount; variation
例句： The company needs to hedge against unexpected fluctuations in raw material prices.
【翻譯】公司必須規避原物料價格非預期的劇烈波動風險。
【高頻考法】market fluctuation (市場波動)
vulnerability /ˌvʌlnərəˈbɪləti/
名詞 · 弱點、漏洞、脆弱性
A flaw or weakness in software or hardware that can be exploited by an attacker
例句： Security engineers patched the critical system vulnerability before hackers could exploit it.
【翻譯】資安工程師在黑客利用漏洞之前，修補了該關鍵系統弱點。
【高頻考法】vulnerability assessment (弱點掃描/評估)
integrity /ɪnˈtɛɡrəti/
名詞 · 誠信正直、資料完整性
The quality of being honest and fair; the state of being complete and unimpaired
例句： Corporate integrity and data accuracy are vital to maintaining public trust.
【翻譯】企業誠信與資料正確性對於維繫社會大眾之信任至關重要。
【高頻考法】data integrity (資料完整性 - 資訊安全CIA三要素之一)
redundancy /rɪˈdʌndənsi/
名詞 · 備援、多餘重疊
The inclusion of extra components functioning in case of failure; state of being unnecessary
例句： Hardware redundancy ensures that the server cluster remains active even during power outages.
【翻譯】硬體備援能確保伺服器叢集即使在停電期間仍能維持運作。
【高頻考法】data redundancy (資料重複 - 資料庫正規化所欲消除者)

📐 台酒英文 十大必考文法公式手冊

TEN CORE GRAMMAR RULES WITH EXAMPLES
文法考點 公式原則 考題例句 (附發音) 繁體中文解析
1. 假設語氣 (與過去相反) If + S + had + p.p., S + would/could + have + p.p. If we had tested the backup server yesterday, the outage wouldn't have caused such data loss. 過去未發生的假設：如果昨天有測試備援伺服器，斷電就不會造成如此嚴重的資料損失。
2. 堅持/要求/命令 (that + S + (should) + 原形V) demand / insist / recommend / require + that + S + (should) + V The security policy requires that every employee change their password every ninety days. 動詞 require 後接 that 子句，動詞一律用原形（省去 should），注意是 change 而非 changes。
3. 否定副詞放句首倒裝 Negative Adverb (Hardly/Scarcely/Seldom/Not only) + 助動詞 + S + V Seldom have we witnessed such a dramatic breakthrough in artificial intelligence. 否定詞 Seldom 放句首，助動詞 have 移到主詞 we 之前形成倒裝。
4. 分詞構句 (省略連接詞與相同主詞) 主動 V-ing / 被動 p.p. , S + V Having completed the database migration, the IT team monitored the query logs closely. 動作先後順序：完成資料庫遷移之後（Having completed），資訊團隊密切監控查詢日誌。
5. 介系詞後接動名詞 (V-ing) look forward to / be committed to / with a view to + V-ing The management is dedicated to improving product quality and reducing carbon footprint. to 為介系詞，後面必須接動名詞 improving，不可接原形動詞 improve。

🎯 歷屆精選模擬測驗（即點即對答案 · 附整題發音與詳解）

PRACTICE QUESTIONS WITH INSTANT GRADING
第 1 題 · 單字克漏
The corporation decided to ________ its product line in order to remain competitive in the global market.
【正確答案】：(B) diversify
【繁體中文翻譯】： 該企業決定將其產品線「多角化」，以在國際市場中保持競爭力。
【選項詞彙解析】：
(A) deteriorate（惡化、退化）
(B) diversify（多角化、使多樣化）➔ 正確選項，符合作為酒品之外拓展生技、化妝品之經營策略
(C) liquidate（清算、變現破產）
(D) contaminate（污染、弄髒）
第 2 題 · 文法與假設語氣
Had the system administrator applied the security patch in time, the database server ________ by ransomware.
【正確答案】：(C) would not have been compromised
【繁體中文翻譯】： 如果系統管理員及時套用資安修補程式，資料庫伺服器就不會遭受勒索軟體攻陷。
【文法解析】：
句首 Had the system administrator applied 為過去假設語氣省略 If 的倒裝句型（原為 If the system administrator had applied...）。
主要子句對應過去事實的假設結果，公式必須使用 would/could + have + p.p.，且伺服器是被入侵，故採用被動態 would not have been compromised。
第 3 題 · 職場商務情境
The manager insists that the audit report ________ before Friday afternoon.
【正確答案】：(A) be submitted
【繁體中文翻譯】： 經理堅持稽核報告必須在週五下午前呈交。
【文法解析】：
動詞 insist（堅持）引導的名詞子句表達要求，子句動詞省略 should 而使用原形動詞。報告為被呈交，故為 (should) be submitted，選 (A)。
