第 4 章　科技類閱讀測驗長難句結構與五步破題法

科技類文章通常是「說明文 (Expository)」或「論說文 (Argumentative)」，常會先介紹一個新技術，接著說明其優點，最後討論其隱憂或法規挑戰。

🎯 步驟化詳解：國考長篇科技閱讀測驗「五步破題法」
  • 步驟 1先讀題幹提取定位詞 (Locators)
    切忌一開始就埋頭讀長文！先看題目選項，圈出大寫專有名詞、年代數字、技術名稱（如 "Zero Trust"、"NIST"、"2023"）。這些字在文章中像螢光筆一樣容易秒找。
  • 步驟 2首尾段快速略讀掌握文章主旨 (Main Idea)
    英文說理文章通常為「倒三角形結構」：
    ‧ 第一段最後一句通常是中心論點 (Thesis Statement)。
    ‧ 最後一段通常是作者立場與總結建議（包含 "Ultimately", "In conclusion" 等標誌詞）。
  • 步驟 3長難句主幹剝離分析 (S + V + O)
    科技文章充斥動輒 30~40 個單字的複合句。破解法：
    ‧ 找出真正的主要子句主詞與述語動詞。
    ‧ 將介系詞片語、關係代名詞子句（who, which, that）、分詞構句（V-ing, V-ed）用括號先刮起來，句子結構立即一目了然！
  • 步驟 4標記轉折詞 (Transitions) 捕捉命題落點
    國考出題老師最喜歡在對比轉折處出題：
    ‧ 看到 However, Nevertheless, In contrast, Despite 立即提高警覺，轉折詞之後的敘述 90% 是考點！
  • 步驟 5選項干擾陷阱逐一剔除 (Debunking)
    ‧ 過度推論 (Over-generalization)：選項出現 always, never, completely 等絕對字眼多為錯誤。
    ‧ 偷換概念 / 因果倒置 (Reverse Causality)：A 導致 B 被竄改成 B 導致 A。
    ‧ 未提及細節 (Not Mentioned)：常理雖正確，但文章根本沒寫，切勿主觀帶入。
⚡ 考前 30 秒秒殺口訣卡：閱讀測驗四大破題秘訣
【口訣】題幹關鍵先定位，首尾兩段抓主旨；主謂抽離破長句，轉折之後藏答案
  • 轉折詞：However, While, Yet, Nonetheless（重點在後句）。
  • 因果詞：Therefore, Consequently, Accordingly（後面接結論）。
  • 遞進詞：Furthermore, In addition, Moreover（列舉同向論點）。

第 5 章　實戰演練：長篇閱讀逐句拆解與選項破解

5-1　實戰文章：零信任與生成式 AI 的資安防禦

📖 原文長篇閱讀 (Zero Trust & AI in Cybersecurity)

The transition toward decentralized cloud infrastructures has fundamentally undermined the efficacy of traditional perimeter-based cybersecurity models. In conventional network architectures, internal traffic was implicitly trusted once authentication occurred at the perimeter firewall. However, the proliferation of sophisticated ransomware and advanced persistent threats (APTs) has necessitated the implementation of the Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA), which operates on the core axiom of "never trust, always verify." Under this framework, every access request—regardless of whether it originates from inside or outside the corporate intranet—must be dynamically validated through multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege authorization policies.

Furthermore, the advent of generative artificial intelligence has introduced a dual-edged sword into enterprise security operations. On the one hand, malicious actors increasingly leverage large language models to construct hyper-realistic spear-phishing campaigns and synthesize polymorphic malware that eludes conventional signature-based intrusion detection systems. On the other hand, security analysts deploy machine learning algorithms to automate real-time anomaly detection and expedite incident response. Ultimately, establishing a resilient cyber posture requires organizations not only to modernize their architectural paradigms through Zero Trust, but also to proactively cultivate cognitive agility among their cybersecurity workforce.

🎯 步驟化詳解：長難句語法樹結構與從屬子句行行拆解
  • 句子 1長句拆解：第一段第四句
    "However, the proliferation of sophisticated ransomware and advanced persistent threats (APTs) [主詞] has necessitated [主要動詞] the implementation of the Zero Trust Architecture (ZTA) [受詞], which operates on the core axiom of 'never trust, always verify' [非限定關係代名詞子句修飾 ZTA]."
    ‧ 主詞 (Subject)：the proliferation of...（精細勒索軟體與進階持續性威脅的激增）。
    ‧ 及物動詞 (Verb)：has necessitated（使得...成為必要）。
    ‧ 受詞 (Object)：the implementation of the Zero Trust Architecture（零信任架構的落實）。
    ‧ 後位修飾：which operates... 說明零信任的核心公理為「永不信任，始終驗證」。
  • 句子 2長句拆解：第二段最後一句（全文結論）
    "Ultimately, establishing a resilient cyber posture [動名詞當主詞] requires [主要動詞] organizations [受詞] not only to modernize their architectural paradigms through Zero Trust, but also to proactively cultivate cognitive agility among their cybersecurity workforce [受詞補語並列]."
    ‧ 句型結構：require + O + not only to A, but also to B（要求受詞不僅要A，而且要B）。
    ‧ 核心意思：建立具備韌性的資安態勢，不僅需要藉由零信任現代化架構，更必須主動培養資安團隊的認知敏捷度！
🎯 步驟化詳解：考題實戰演練與干擾選項精準破解

【國考模擬試題】According to the passage, which of the following statements is TRUE?
(A) Traditional perimeter firewalls are fully capable of mitigating advanced persistent threats.
(B) Zero Trust Architecture assumes all internal network traffic is trustworthy by default.
(C) Generative AI exclusively benefits cyber attackers by generating undetectable malware.
(D) Achieving a resilient cyber defense necessitates both architectural evolution and human skill enhancement.

  • 破解 A選項 (A) 錯誤（與原文相反）
    第一段指出傳統邊界模型已被嚴重破壞（fundamentally undermined），無法抵禦 APT 威脅。
  • 破解 B選項 (B) 錯誤（偷換觀念）
    零信任原則是「never trust, always verify」，預設不信任任何內部流量。
  • 破解 C選項 (C) 錯誤（過度極端字詞 "exclusively"）
    第二段指出 AI 是一把雙面刃（dual-edged sword），防守方分析師也使用機器學習來進行異常偵測。
  • 正解 D選項 (D) 正確（答案：D）
    與最後一句 "not only to modernize their architectural paradigms... but also to proactively cultivate... workforce" 意義完全契合！
